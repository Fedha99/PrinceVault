# Constraint Intel HD 4000

GPU integrated Ivy Bridge ini ngebatesin banyak keputusan ricing & gaming di [[Spek HP Pavilion g4]].

## Batasan yang Udah Established
- **No blur** di Hyprland compositor.
- **No video wallpaper** (mpvpaper) — terlalu berat.
- **No high shadow render_power**.

## Dampak ke Area Lain
- Jadi alasan utama upgrade ke [[ThinkPad T14 Gen 2 AMD]].
- Jadi motivasi [[Benchmark Kernel Zen CachyOS Xanmod]] buat cari tiap ons performa ekstra buat [[Modpack Forge 1.20.1 Overview|Minecraft modpack]].
- Baseline performa modpack di HD 4000: **65 FPS** post-fix (Forge), **~120 FPS** pake Fabric performance stack.
