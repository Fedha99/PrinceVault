## Definisi
Bitwarden adalah password manager open-source, zero-knowledge, enkripsi AES-256. Gratis unlimited device & sync. Fitur: Password Generator, Auto-fill (Ctrl+Shift+L), 2FA TOTP, Passkeys support, Vault Health Reports (Premium US$10/tahun). Master Password = kunci utama (hilang = data tak terpulihkan).
