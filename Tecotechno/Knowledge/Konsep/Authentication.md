## Definisi
Authentication (authN) = verifikasi identitas claimant ("kamu siapa"). Berbeda dari Authorization (authZ = "kamu boleh apa"). 3 Faktor: Knowledge (password), Possession (HP/token), Inherence (biometrik). MFA = ≥2 faktor berbeda. Passkeys = passwordless public-key crypto, phishing-resistant. Serangan: credential stuffing, phishing, brute force, session hijack.
