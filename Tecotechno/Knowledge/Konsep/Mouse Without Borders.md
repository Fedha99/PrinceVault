## Definisi
Mouse Without Borders (Microsoft PowerToys) = kontrol hingga 4 PC Windows dengan 1 mouse/keyboard lewat jaringan lokal. Security Key pairing, virtual monitor positioning, Share Clipboard (copy-paste lintas PC), transfer file, Wrap Mouse. Hanya Windows, butuh jaringan stabil (LAN preferred).
