## Definisi
RIP (Routing Information Protocol) protokol distance vector klasik (1988). Metric = hop count (max 15). Router hanya tahu info tetangga langsung. Update berkala 30 detik. Rentan routing loop → mitigasi: split horizon, poison reverse, holddown timer. Cocok jaringan kecil/lab belajar fundamental.
