## Ringkasan
Pengantar MikroTik: perusahaan Latvia (hardware RouterBOARD + software RouterOS), sejarah 1995, kenapa populer (murah, fitur enterprise). 10 cara belajar: paham hardware/software, install CHR di GNS3, kuasai Winbox/CLI, pelajari IP/routing dasar, praktek firewall/NAT, DHCP/hotspot, monitoring (The Dude, Zabbix), sertifikasi MTCNA, join komunitas, bangun lab rumah.

## Poin Penting
- MikroTik = RouterBOARD (hardware) + RouterOS (software) — mirip HP + Android/iOS
- Murah tapi fitur setara enterprise (routing, firewall, wireless, VPN, hotspot, monitoring)
- RouterOS v7: CLI powerful, Winbox GUI, API, scripting
- 10 cara belajar: (1) Kenal hardware/software, (2) CHR di GNS3, (3) Winbox/CLI, (4) IP/routing, (5) Firewall/NAT, (6) DHCP/Hotspot, (7) Monitoring, (8) MTCNA, (9) Komunitas, (10) Lab rumah
- CHR (Cloud Hosted Router) gratis untuk virtualisasi (GNS3, VMware, VirtualBox)

## Langkah / Cara Kerja
1. Download CHR image dari mikrotik.com → import ke GNS3/VMware
2. Akses via Winbox (MAC/IP) atau SSH/CLI
3. Pelajari `/ip address`, `/ip route`, `/ip firewall`, `/ip dhcp-server`, `/ip hotspot`
4. Ikuti materi MTCNA 9 modul (routing, wireless, firewall, dll)
5. Gabung forum/group MikroTik Indonesia; bangun lab fisik/virtual
